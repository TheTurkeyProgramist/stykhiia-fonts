Stykhiia Text is a glyphic sans serif with flared stroke endings, made for Stykhiia, a Ukrainian weather website. Its forms are bent gently, as if by wind: diagonals are slightly concave, arches lean to the right, crossbars sit high and the top of Т spreads like a wing.

The family covers GF Latin Core and GF Cyrillic Core, including Ukrainian, Russian, Belarusian, Bulgarian, Serbian, Macedonian, Kazakh and other Cyrillic orthographies, plus Latin Extended-A. Figures are proportional by default, with tabular figures available via the tnum feature.
